The checked-in snapshot is redistributed with source attribution and the limitations summarized in [DATA_SOURCES.md](DATA_SOURCES.md):

- RIGIS rescinded its historical restrictive license effective September 1, 2014. Its replacement notice permits distribution while asking derived products to acknowledge RIGIS and the primary producer and disclaims warranties.
- USFWS and NPS materials produced by federal employees are generally public-domain U.S. government works, but source-layer metadata, attribution, accuracy limitations, third-party content, names, and marks still apply. This project preserves agency attribution and does not use agency logos.
- No separate data license was found in the public POTA API or documentation reviewed for this release. The catalog therefore republishes only the minimal factual reference metadata needed for interoperability, provides an official link for every record, identifies POTA as the authoritative current source, and makes no claim over POTA names or marks.

Downstream users are responsible for checking the linked current source terms and for preserving attribution and disclaimers. The snapshot does not grant access to any property, establish ownership, or determine whether a location is valid or open for activation.
