source page, exact downloaded source URL and digest, reviewed rights, and
transformations.
These rights apply to the shipped WebP and remain separate from the MIT code
license. No protection is claimed in original U.S. Government works. An agency
website alone is not proof that a third-party photograph is public domain.

Preserve the creator credit, supplied title/caption, source link, license link,
and modification notices when redistributing or displaying photos. This package's
orientation, resizing, and WebP encoding are recorded in `transforms`; disclose
additional crops or edits in downstream attribution. Contributions to the
included CC BY-SA photographs are distributed under each image's listed
share-alike license. Retain that license for your derivatives too. CC BY photos
retain their listed attribution license. Public-domain and CC0 records retain
their source acknowledgments for provenance.
